Questions

Do I have to pay Google too?

No. Hugo uses your own free Google AI key, which takes 2 minutes to make when you first open Hugo (the setup window shows you how). Google's free allowance is plenty for normal daily use.

Is my screen private?

Hugo only looks when you ask: then a picture of what you boxed (or your screen) goes to Google's AI to answer it. Nothing is sent while Hugo is idle, and your key, notes and settings stay on your PC.

Which computers does it work on?

Windows 10 and 11 PCs and laptops. The first start downloads Hugo's voice ears (about 500 MB, once). Not available for Mac or phones yet.

Who is Hugo for?

College students, people preparing for exams, and anyone who reads, studies or works on a PC. Google's AI is for people 18 and over, so Hugo is too.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

Yes. Hugo is new, so Windows doesn't recognise it yet. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

What if Pro doesn't work for me?

Try everything free for 7 days first. If Hugo Pro doesn't work on your PC, message within 3 days of paying for a full refund.

Will the answers always be right?

Hugo checks every Solve page with a second, independent solve and marks it ✓ checked, or asks you to double-check when the two don't agree. Like any AI it can make mistakes, so use it to learn, not to copy.
